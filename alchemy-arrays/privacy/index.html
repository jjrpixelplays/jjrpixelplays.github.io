Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Last updated: October 15, 2026

In short: Alchemy Arrays does not ask for, collect, or share your personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. The only things the game saves are your settings, such as your chosen color theme, and they stay on your device.

1About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Alchemy Arrays (“the App”, “the game”), developed and published by Janak Rana (“we”, “us”, “our”), handles information when you use the App on Android, iOS, and tablet devices.

By downloading or using the App, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2Information we collect

We do not collect any personally identifiable information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal details, and it does not require you to create an account.

Information stored only on your device

To remember your preferences between sessions, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device using the operating system’s standard app storage:

DataWhy it is savedWhere it is kept
Selected color theme (Light Green, Light Orange, or Light Pale Yellow)So the game opens in the theme you pickedYour device only
Game settings and preferencesSo your choices are kept the next time you playYour device only

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your identity.

3Device permissions

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, storage, or other sensitive device features. It works fully offline.

4Third-party services

The App is distributed through the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. When you download or update the App, those platforms may collect information according to their own privacy policies, such as download statistics and anonymous crash reports that you have allowed in your device settings. We do not control this collection.

  • Apple Privacy Policy

The App itself does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it does not sell or share data with third parties.

5Children’s privacy

Alchemy Arrays is a puzzle game suitable for players of all ages. Because the App does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has somehow provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6Data security

Your preferences are kept in the App’s private storage on your device, which other apps cannot normally access. Since we do not transmit or store your data on any server, there is no data on our side that could be exposed.

7Deleting your data

You are always in control of the data the App saves. To remove it, you can either clear the App’s data from your device settings or uninstall the App. Both actions permanently delete your saved theme and settings.

8Your rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as the GDPR (European Union), the CCPA (California), or India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, may give you rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Because we do not collect or hold personal data about you, there is nothing for us to provide or remove, but you are welcome to contact us with any questions.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if new features are added to the game. When we do, we will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will describe it clearly here before it takes effect. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
