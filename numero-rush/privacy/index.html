Numero Rush

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Numero Rush (“the Game”, “we”, “us”), a dice-based number board game developed by Janak Rana, handles information when you use the Game on Android or iOS devices.

In short: Numero Rush does not collect, store on any server, share, or sell any personal information. The Game works fully offline, and everything you enter stays on your device.

1Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data.

2Information stored on your device

To make the Game work, some information is saved locally on your device only. This information is never sent to us or to any third party:

  • Player names you type in when setting up a game (these can be any nickname and do not need to be real names)
  • Your chosen props (faces, fruits, animals, balls, colours) and selected themes
  • Game settings, saved progress, results, and leaderboard / high-score entries

Because this data stays on your device, we cannot see it, access it, or recover it.

3Internet access and permissions

Numero Rush is designed to be played offline. The Game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or files, and it does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

4Advertising and analytics

The Game does not display advertisements and does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

5Third-party services

The Game is distributed through the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store. When you download or update the Game, these platforms may collect information (such as device type and crash reports) under their own privacy policies, which we do not control:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6Children’s privacy

Numero Rush is suitable for players of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13, no personal information from children is ever collected or shared. Parents can supervise player names entered in the Game, which are stored on the device only.

7Data retention and deletion

All Game data remains on your device until you remove it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app’s data in your device settings or by uninstalling the Game.

8Security

Since your Game data never leaves your device, it is protected by your device’s own security features. We encourage you to keep your device and operating system up to date.

9Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU or the DPDP Act in India), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since we do not collect or hold any personal data, there is nothing for us to access or delete; you are in full control of the data on your device.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if new features are added. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the Game after changes means you accept the updated policy.
